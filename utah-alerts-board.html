Coverage note: This board currently pulls the National Weather Service feed for Utah, which covers weather and water alerts plus NWS-relayed emergencies. It does not yet capture every IPAWS pathway — a county alert sent to WEA or EAS without going through NWS will not appear here. WEA coverage via PBS WARN and the full IPAWS All-Hazards Information Feed are planned additions. For official warnings, always follow Wireless Emergency Alerts, the Emergency Alert System, and NOAA Weather Radio. Alert data is public information served by the National Weather Service; this board only displays it.
